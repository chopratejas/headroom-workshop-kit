## Known product issues seen while building this (0.41.0)

- With Kompress loaded (local or hosted), raw logs go through the relevance split and Kompress: 9% saved instead of 98% from the log compressor, which is never consulted. The upside: the root-cause INFO line survives.
- Without Kompress, the log compressor drops every INFO line on a page, including the root-cause line, and the `# page N of M` header. A page with one ERROR line is not compressed at all.
- With `HEADROOM_KOMPRESS_ENDPOINT` set, the proxy still downloads the 262 MB local model at startup.
- The dashboard headline includes a deferred-tools estimate (`tool_saved`); use the `Pipeline complete` log lines for compression.
